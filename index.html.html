The Author

Donald Bowen

Writer of the Aegis Cycle

Donald Bowen holds a doctorate in criminal justice and a master's degree in sociology — a background that shows up throughout the Aegis Cycle less as research than as instinct: an eye for how institutions protect themselves, how secrecy calcifies into policy, and how the people inside a system learn to call its costs by gentler names.

The Aegis Cycle began as a single question — what does an organization become after it has kept the same secret for a hundred years — and grew into a series about the machinery, human and otherwise, that a society builds to survive, and what that machinery quietly demands in return.
